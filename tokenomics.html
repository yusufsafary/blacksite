How a pool drains

Each declassified dossier pays 3,000,000 $SITE, split by shares across registered fragments. A standard fragment holds one share; a Keystone holds five. Shares nobody claims before a window closes are burned, along with every fragment of a failed dossier. At three dossiers a day, a 2,000,000,000 pool covers roughly 222 days of full completions.
